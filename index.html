1. Introduction

Welcome to POWER OUTAGE AI assistant ("Application", "we", "our", or "us").

POWER OUTAGE AI assistant is an AI-powered customer support application that receives and responds to customer inquiries through WhatsApp. It helps customers report power outages, request electricity interruption information, and receive automated responses.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, where applicable.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, share, and protect personal information when you interact with our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • WhatsApp phone number.
  • WhatsApp profile name, where available.
  • Customer name, if provided.
  • Electricity meter or customer account number, if provided.
  • Location or service area, if shared.
  • Other information voluntarily provided in messages.

2.2 Communication Data

  • Incoming and outgoing WhatsApp messages.
  • Message timestamps.
  • Message delivery and processing statuses, where available.
  • Conversation history and context.
  • Attachments submitted through supported channels.

2.3 Power Outage Information

  • Reported outage locations.
  • Affected areas and electricity distribution zones.
  • Transformer, feeder, or network identifiers, if provided.
  • Reported outage dates and times.
  • Descriptions of electricity interruptions.
  • Related complaints and service requests.

2.4 Technical Information

  • Application and system logs.
  • API request and response metadata.
  • Error reports and diagnostic information.
  • AI processing records.
  • Security and performance monitoring information.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless it is voluntarily provided and necessary for a legitimate service purpose. Please avoid sending passwords, payment credentials, or unrelated sensitive information through WhatsApp.

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information through:

  • WhatsApp messages: When you send a message to our designated WhatsApp number, we receive the message and associated information through the WhatsApp Business Platform, directly or through an authorized provider.
  • Automated conversations: Our AI assistant processes messages and relevant conversation context to generate responses.
  • Service integrations: We may receive information from authorized operational systems where necessary to investigate outages or respond to inquiries.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Respond to customer inquiries and provide assistance.
  • Receive, categorize, and process power outage reports.
  • Retrieve relevant outage information from connected systems.
  • Analyze messages and generate AI-powered responses.
  • Route complaints to appropriate teams where integrated.
  • Improve response accuracy and service reliability.
  • Detect abuse and protect system security.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information or use customer conversations for unrelated advertising.

5. AI Processing and Automated Responses

POWER OUTAGE AI assistant uses artificial intelligence to understand customer messages and generate responses.

  1. Your incoming message is received through WhatsApp.
  2. The application processes your message and relevant context.
  3. The AI system interprets the request.
  4. Relevant information may be retrieved from connected sources.
  5. An automated response is generated and sent through WhatsApp.

AI-generated responses may contain errors, incomplete information, or outdated details. The assistant should not be treated as an authoritative source for emergencies or guaranteed electricity restoration times.

Depending on our configuration, messages may be processed by third-party AI providers. Such providers process information according to applicable service agreements and safeguards.

We do not authorize unrelated AI model training using customer information unless separately disclosed and supported by an appropriate lawful basis.
